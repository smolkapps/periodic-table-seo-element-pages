## ChemEd X

**Routes:** [Contribute](https://www.chemedx.org/contribute), [current Contribution Guidelines](https://www.chemedx.org/contribution-guidelines), and the public [Contact form](https://www.chemedx.org/contact). Contributions are editorially reviewed. Articles can discuss useful teaching resources; Activities expect actual use with students. Picks should come from an instructor who has used the resource, and the guidelines generally exclude Picks written by the resource creator. A developer pitch must disclose that relationship. [Guidelines](https://www.chemedx.org/contribution-guidelines)

The public contact form has required name, email, subject and message fields, with Preview/Send controls. This is a suitability inquiry, not automatic publication. The contribution route requires a free member account, a completed author profile and the official manuscript template. No author fee was stated on the inspected pages; that doesn't prove every publication service is free. [Contact](https://www.chemedx.org/contact), [contribution route](https://www.chemedx.org/contribute)

Public [registration](https://www.chemedx.org/user/register) shows first/last name, email, summary bio, affiliation, chemistry-education role, professional-verification URL, headshot, tagline and optional additional profile details. Name/email/summary are marked required. A contributor's photo/tagline become public; the downstream manuscript-upload screen remains uninspected. Use Developer/Other honestly rather than invent a teaching position.
